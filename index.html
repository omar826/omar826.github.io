About

I am an undergraduate at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), specializing in Mathematics and Computing. I’m interested in mathematical logic, automata theory, programming languages, and formal methods, mostly as ways of understanding computation through precise mathematics.

I’m currently enjoying designing proof search techniques: deciding what a prover should try first, what it should never try, and what a logic has to look like for the search to stand a chance. I’ve also worked on synthesis bottlenecks that limit fully automated program verification, such as generating loop invariants or library contracts, approached with neuro-symbolic methods.

Some of my motivation is practical. Now that AI writes so much of our code, checking that code has become the harder problem, and I’d like verification to be something programmers simply do as part of writing software. Getting there will take better proof automation, and also languages in which specifications and proofs are easier to write in the first place. Mostly, though, I just enjoy it. Lately I’ve been reading a lot about arguments built on self-reference.

Research

Grounded Deduction

Summer Research Fellow · EPFL, Switzerland · May 2026 – Present (onsite May – July 2026; remote after)

Adviser: Prof. Bryan Ford, EPFL

  • Developing Grounded Deduction, a formal reasoning framework that admits arbitrary recursive definitions without prior termination proofs, using explicit definedness conditions to reason about potentially nonterminating computations.
  • Building on an existing Isabelle/Pure implementation of Grounded Deduction, encoded Basic Grounded Arithmetic’s syntax, semantics, and proof checker and proved its consistency within GD, using step-indexed satisfaction.
  • Developing GD2, a usability-focused implementation of Grounded Deduction in Isabelle/Pure, with unified terms and formulas, first-class functions, and a definable fixed-point operator supporting recursive and mutually recursive definitions without new axioms or termination proofs at definition time.
  • Adapting and extending proof automation and libraries, with comparative case studies in Isabelle/HOL and Lean 4; developing a hammer that translates GD2 goals to first-order problems and reconstructs external proofs in Isabelle.
  • Extended GD’s mechanized metatheory by adding proof-checker rules and proving primitive recursiveness and truth preservation; proved that Propositional Grounded Arithmetic (PGA) contains its own truth predicate.

A consistency proof for BGA carried out inside grounded arithmetic in Isabelle/Pure

Technical report · October 2026

  • Existing mechanized consistency results for grounded arithmetic are carried out in Isabelle/HOL, a classical metatheory far stronger than the object logic. This report carries the argument out inside a version of grounded arithmetic itself, proving as a theorem of QGA that BGA cannot prove both a = b and a ≠ b from empty hypotheses.
